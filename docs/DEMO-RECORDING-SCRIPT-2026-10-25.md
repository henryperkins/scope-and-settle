## Recording and editorial acceptance

1. Record the actual current hosted build. Start with an isolated run and preserve its private recovery link outside the capture. Do not repeat inference or payment solely for additional camera angles when the verified state can be shown truthfully.
2. Keep the main request, quote, approval, invoice and Paid screens on **one actual order and revision**. The over-limit example must carry a visible “Separate limit-check run” label. Do not splice an earlier paid order into the new automatic run.
3. Show authorization and client consent as separate actions. Label condensed waiting/navigation. If manual refresh produces the first Paid state, say so; only claim signed webhook delivery when the actual event is independently verified.
4. Capture browser and, if used, desktop Figma interaction on the actual device. Fixture output, simulated payments and restored screenshots cannot stand in for execution.
5. Exclude judging codes, role URLs, passwords, provider keys, email addresses and private account details. Inspect every scene and transition for accidental exposure. Use opaque redactions where needed.
6. Record and export narration/captions after the actual result is known. Verify pronunciation, readable amounts/revision labels, full playback, English caption timing and an exported duration below 180 seconds. No unlicensed music or assets.
7. Upload the reviewed video publicly to YouTube, verify signed-out playback, and only then replace the Devpost video URL. Preserve the old video's dated evidence and identify which release the new footage demonstrates.

## Operator checklist after billing is restored

- Resume the preserved failed request and verify that one fresh assessment produces the real $250 automatic quote. Record its order ID and revision privately with the invoice evidence.
- Explicit client consent → real PayPal sandbox invoice → dedicated buyer checkout → authoritative Paid / $0 due → same revision after reopening.
- Independently record any signed PAID callback and its reconciliation timing; do not infer it from a manual refresh.
- Run the separate $200 limit example without another buyer payment.
- Record the actual source/version, artifact digest, timestamps and final public video URL in the readiness record. Retain the failed billing attempts as dated evidence.
